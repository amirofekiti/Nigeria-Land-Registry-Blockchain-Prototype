# Nigeria Land Registry Blockchain Prototype

Research prototype supporting the manuscript **Design and Experimental Evaluation of a Blockchain-Assisted Land Administration Framework for Secure and Auditable Property Registration in Nigeria**.

This project treats blockchain as an **audit, provenance and workflow-integrity layer**, not as a substitute for the statutory land registry. It does not treat an NFT or wallet address as legal land title.

## Research status

The current artefact has completed:

- functional and negative-path validation;
- deterministic property/invariant testing;
- Slither static analysis and remediation;
- controlled local benchmarking on the final contract;
- aligned 10-repetition Ethereum Sepolia evaluation on the final contract.

Primary manuscript evidence and provenance are recorded in `docs/phase3-aligned-evaluation.md` and `docs/phase2d-security-results.md`.

The prototype is a research artefact and is **not** represented as legally authoritative, production-secure or ready for live land records.
